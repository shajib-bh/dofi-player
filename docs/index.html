Why the extra click? Dofi is free and independent, so it skips Apple's $99 a year notarization. You approve it once. Updates never ask again.

Quick answers.

Is Dofi really free?

Yes. No price, no ads, no account, no trial that runs out.

Is it safe?

There's no tracking and no account. Dofi only goes online to check for updates, search subtitles you ask for, and play links you paste. The download comes straight from GitHub.

Will it run on my Mac?

If your Mac has an M1 chip or newer and macOS 14 Sonoma or later, yes. Intel Macs aren't supported yet.

Can I keep VLC or IINA?

Of course. Try Dofi on your next movie. If you love it, right-click any video, choose Get Info, set Open with to Dofi Player and click Change All.
