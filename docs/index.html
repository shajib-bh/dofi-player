Install in a minute

Dofi is made by an independent developer and isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS asks you to confirm once. Here is exactly what to do.

  1. Download the latest .dmg from the releases page and open it.
  2. Drag Dofi Player into Applications in the window that opens.
  3. Open Dofi Player. macOS says it could not verify the app. Click Done (not Move to Trash).
  4. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway next to Dofi Player and confirm with your password.
  5. That's it. You only do this once. Future updates install with one click and open straight away.

Questions

Short answers to what people ask most.

Is Dofi Player free?

Yes. It's free to download and use, with no ads, no account and no tracking. Dofi only goes online to check for updates, search subtitles you ask for, and play links you paste.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) running macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Intel Macs are not supported yet.

Why does macOS say "Apple could not verify" Dofi Player?

Apple shows this for apps that aren't notarized through its paid developer program. Follow the install steps above: click Done, then Open Anyway in Privacy & Security. You only need to do it once.

How do I download subtitles?

Open a video and press ⌘F. Dofi fills in the title for you, you pick a language, and one click loads the subtitle. You can also drop your own SRT, ASS or VTT file onto the window.

How is it different from VLC or IINA?

Like IINA, Dofi is built on the mpv engine, so it plays almost any format. Dofi adds a built-in online subtitle search, fully styled subtitles, playback across several displays and a modern Liquid Glass look.

How do updates work?

Dofi checks for a new version once a day. When one is out, click Install & Restart and you're on the latest version a few seconds later.
